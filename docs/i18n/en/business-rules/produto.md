# Produto

This page is the index of the `produto` area business rules. The rules live in parts (`produto/parte-NN.md`), cut by range so the page opens light; a new rule goes into the LAST part (ADR 0287).

| Part | From | Rules | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| [part 1](produto/parte-01.md) | RN-012 | 13 | Backlog; Projects panel; "N agents online" on the dashboard — LIVE status, never historical presence (RN-409, ADR 0097); The Runner's folder explorer becomes three columns, and creating a `runner` project no longer waits for the final step (RN-436/436); Local runner terminal stuck at "Opening terminal..." forever (RN-433); RAG search leaves a trace, and the trace freezes the weights (RN-479..481); PHASE 29 — the one-line installation: signing what is published; The agents' artifacts become files, in `docs/` (RN-523) |
| [part 2](produto/parte-02.md) | RN-543 | 9 | The device key can now belong to the MACHINE (RN-543, PHASE 30); An installation's first account is born in the terminal (RN-546); `install.sh` closes the installation (RN-547, PHASE 30); The self-movement ceiling on the WORKSPACE upsert, and the self-promotion that closes (RN-557); The device key gets a screen, and the screen states the reach of revoking (RN-561); The container broker in the installation, with consent (RN-575); The installer offers the local models, and does not mistake dev for an installation (RN-691, RN-692); The installer's migration brings the whole previous installation back (RN-998) |
