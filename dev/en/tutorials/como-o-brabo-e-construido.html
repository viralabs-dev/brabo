    How Brabo is built

    Who it's for: anyone who wants to replicate Brabo's way of working in their own product, with or without AI agents. You don't need to know the project's history.

    After reading, you can:

    • set up a board with one epic and two tasks, one of them ready to start;
    • run a round with discovery and execution that ends in a PR;
    • tie each business rule to a file and a test;
    • copy the templates and the sample agents.

    Reading time: about 20 minutes; each page takes 3 to 5.

    Structure and method

    The path Orchestrating a product across many repositories teaches the structure: how to split the product and how the repositories connect. This one teaches the method: how work comes in, moves and goes out. Anyone who copies only the structure ends up with eleven repositories and no rhythm.

    The examples here are made up and neutral. Where there is real proof, the text links to a public PR, such as PR 79 of brabo, which published the structure path.

    The order of the pages

    # Page Question it answers
    1 From request to task How does a request become work someone can start?
    2 The round How does work move, with people and agents?
    3 The rule becomes code, and the docs go with it How does a decision leave a trace in the code and the docs?
    4 Quality and the permanent acceptance test How do I know the whole product still works?
    5 Copy the method What do I take from here, ready to use?

    • You want the board and the tasks: read 1 and 5.
    • You want to work with agents: read 2 and 5.
