    Orchestrating a product across many repositories

    Who it is for: anyone who is about to split a product into several repositories, or already has and feels the cost, and does not know Brabo's history. You do not need to have read any ADR or taken part in any round of work: ADRs appear only as "learn more".

    After reading, you can:

    • decide whether your product should really be split, and when it should not;
    • draw the repository map with the consumption mode of each edge;
    • narrate the path of a change, from the first PR to the installer;
    • build a similar set, in the right order, knowing what to copy and what to adapt.

    Reading time: about 40 minutes for the whole path; each page takes 5 to 8.

    The example we use

    Brabo is a platform that orchestrates engineering agents. It lives in 11 public repositories in the viralabs-dev organization. None of them is "the" product: the product is the set, and one repository, brabo, is the front door. The old single repository is for reference only and is outside this path.

    The order of the pages

    # Page Question it answers
    1 Why split, and when not to Is it worth splitting? What does it cost?
    2 The map Who does what, and who depends on whom?
    3 How each repository is consumed Through what does one repository deliver something to another?
    4 The life of a change How does a line of code become a release?
    5 Build your own In what order do I replicate this?
    6 Mistakes we already made What can I avoid?

    And the way of working?

    This path is the structure. The method that makes it move (board, tasks, rounds with agents, the permanent acceptance test) is in How Brabo is built.

    How to read

    • Deciding whether to split: read 1 and 6.
    • Already split and want to organize: read 2, 3 and 4.
    • Want to get hands-on: go straight to 5, which points to the others when needed.

    Each page says, at the start, who it is for and what you can do after it.

    Where evidence is missing

    Where the path has no evidence to state something, it writes TODO(humano) instead of inventing.
